## Information about the Venue

The conference will be held at the Daejeon Convention Center (DCC) Exhibition Hall 1 in Daejeon, South Korea.
![View of DCC](/assets/images/DCC.jpg)


The DCC is located at 107 Expo-ro, Yuseong-gu, Daejeon, South Korea. For more information about the venue, please visit the [DCC official website](https://www.dcckorea.or.kr/eng/index.do).


### Getting to Daejeon

After arriving at Incheon International Airport (ICN), you can take one of the following options to reach Daejeon:

Routes, timetables, fares, and ticket availability may change. Before traveling, please check the [Incheon International Airport public transportation guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1501/subview.do) and the [KORAIL official booking website](https://korail.go.kr/global/eng/main).

- **KTX Train**: Take the Airport Railroad Express (AREX) from Incheon Airport to Seoul Station, then transfer to a KTX train to Daejeon Station (Seodaejeon Station for some trains). The total travel time is approximately 2.5 to 3 hours.
- **Express Bus**: Direct express buses operate from Incheon Airport to Daejeon. Daejeon Government Complex (대전청사) is the recommended stop. The travel time is approximately 3 to 4 hours, depending on traffic conditions. Please confirm the current timetable and boarding terminal before traveling.


### Getting to DCC
- **Taxi**: Anywhere in Daejeon, you can take a taxi to DCC. Depending on your starting location, the fare is usually between 5,000 and 15,000 KRW. Kakao Taxi is a popular app for hailing taxis in South Korea, and Uber can also be used to call regular taxis. If a taxi driver has difficulty finding the venue, show the Korean name: 대전컨벤션센터.
- **Public Transportation**: Daejeon has one subway line (Line 1) and several bus routes. A reloadable T-money card can be used on subways and buses and recharged at convenience stores or station kiosks. A single ride is typically around 1,300 to 1,700 KRW; transfers made with T-money within 30 minutes are generally free. Please verify current fares, transfer rules, and routes using the [official Daejeon bus guide](https://daejeon.go.kr/tra/TraContentsHtmlView.do?menuSeq=5457) or a current transit app.
    - **From Daejeon Station**: Take Bus No. 707 to DCC (approximately 1 hour), or take the subway to Government Complex Daejeon Station and transfer to Bus No. 618 (approximately 40 minutes).
    - **From Daejeon Government Complex (대전청사)**: Take Bus No. 911 or 618 to DCC (approximately 20 minutes).

For additional current route information, see [How to get to IBS, Daejeon](https://travel.dimag.kr/). IBS is located near DCC, and the guide also includes directions to several nearby hotels.

### Recommended Accommodations

Room rates and availability change. Please check each hotel's official website before booking.

* [Hotel Onoma](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/cjjak-hotel-onoma-daejeon-autograph-collection/overview/): A hotel near Shinsegae Department Store, about a 10-minute walk from DCC. Standard-room prices start at around 300,000 KRW per night. The ASIACOMB 2026 special-rate booking deadline has passed; please check the hotel's website for current rates and availability.
* [LOTTE City Hotel, Daejeon](https://www.lottehotel.com/daejeon-city/en): A hotel near DCC, about a 3-minute walk away. Standard-room prices start at around 180,000 KRW per night.
* [ICC Hotel](http://hotel.hotelicc.com/view/index.do?SS_SVC_LANG_CODE=ENG): A hotel near DCC, about a 3-minute walk away. Standard-room prices start at around 150,000 KRW per night.
* [Toyoko Inn Daejeon Government Complex](https://www.toyoko-inn.com/korea/search/detail/00234/): A hotel near the Daejeon Government Complex, about a 40-minute walk or 20-minute bus ride from DCC. Standard-room prices start at around 80,000 KRW per night.
* [I-Hotel](https://i-hotel.co.kr/): A hotel near DCC, about a 3-minute walk away. Standard-room prices start at around 100,000 KRW per night.
