# The 2026 ASIACOMB Prize

The 2026 ASIACOMB Prize is awarded biennially at the ASIACOMB conference.

The prize recognizes outstanding research contributions in combinatorics by researchers of Asian origin or based in Asia who are not older than 35. It aims to highlight exceptional achievements and to promote the development of combinatorics in the region.

Nominations are now closed. The winner of the 2026 ASIACOMB Prize is:

<p class="prize-winner-name">
	<span class="prize-winner-person">Huy Tuan Pham</span>
	<span class="prize-winner-affiliation">(Caltech)</span>
</p>

The prize will be presented at the Prize Ceremony (Wednesday, August 26, 15:40–16:10) during the conference.
Immediately after the ceremony, the winner will give a one-hour lecture.
